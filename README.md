<h1 align="center">Ikkoma</h1>
<p align="center"><b>One line in. A whole comic out.</b><br>AI comic maker for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.</p>

Ikkoma (一コマ, "one panel") turns one line of story into a comic. Apple Intelligence writes the script on your iPhone, and Ikkoma's own art engine draws every panel on the device, with the same character in every panel.

- No account, no servers, no tracking. Ikkoma collects no data.
- 8 art styles, a page or vertical-strip layout, and real typed lettering.
- 3 comics free, then pay once. No subscription.
